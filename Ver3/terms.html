02Contracting parties

Any person who uses the Hotel's services is a contracting party. The Hotel is not obliged to verify the intermediary's authority to act on the guest's behalf.

03Making a booking

Bookings must be made in writing — via the Hotel's booking engine, an online travel platform or by e-mail — and must state the guest's name, the dates of arrival and departure, the room type and any additional services required.

A contract is concluded when the Hotel confirms the booking in writing. Verbal arrangements do not constitute a binding contract. By making a booking, the guest accepts these Terms, which form an integral part of the contract. Accommodation contracts are concluded for a fixed term.

An extension of the stay is subject to the Hotel's prior consent. Any amendment to a confirmed contract requires the written agreement of both parties.

04Cancellation and no-show

Unless the terms of the booked rate state otherwise:

  • Standard bookings may be cancelled free of charge up to 5 days before the date of arrival.
  • Cancellations made within 5 days of arrival are charged at 100% of the price of the entire booked stay.
  • A no-show is charged at 100% of the price of the entire booked stay.
  • Early departure does not reduce the amount payable; the entire booked stay is charged in full.
  • Non-refundable rates cannot be cancelled free of charge; their own terms apply from the moment of booking.

Cancellations and amendments must be received by the Hotel in writing before the stay begins. Group bookings are subject to the cancellation terms set out in the separate group contract.

Where a refund is due, it will be made to the original method of payment within 10 working days. Refunds to American Express cards may take longer owing to the card provider's different processing procedures.

05Prices

Rates are quoted per room per night and may change over time. The price confirmed in the booking is the price that applies to that booking.

Rates are shown with an indication of whether taxes are included. Unless expressly stated otherwise, the local tourist tax is payable in addition. Should a tax rate be changed by law, the amount payable will change accordingly.

An obvious pricing error — a rate so far below the market level that it could not reasonably be regarded as genuine — is not binding on the Hotel. In such a case, the Hotel will promptly inform the guest of the correct price, and the guest may either keep or cancel the booking.

Discounts and promotions may be published on this website and cannot be combined with any other promotion.

06Payment

Payment is due no later than on arrival. To secure a booking, the Hotel may require a credit card guarantee, or a partial or full advance payment.

Invoices are issued in the currency of payment, which may be either EUR or HUF.

The Hotel accepts cash in HUF and EUR, bank transfer, and all generally accepted debit and credit cards. Any costs associated with the chosen payment method are borne by the guest.

Charges arising after departure — such as minibar consumption, missing or damaged items, or penalties for breach of contract — are invoiced within 72 hours.

07Arrival and departure

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Reception is staffed 24 hours a day, so guests may arrive at any time.

Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability. If a room is not vacated by the agreed time and no extension has been agreed, an additional night may be charged.

Hungarian law requires the Hotel to record a photo identity document for every guest, and to transmit the data to the VIZA system. The Hotel is unable to accommodate any guest who does not present such a document. Information on how this data is handled is set out in our Privacy Policy.

08House rules

Guests are asked to treat the Hotel, its staff and other guests with courtesy, and to keep noise to a minimum at night.

  • Smoking is strictly prohibited in all enclosed areas, including guest rooms, under Act XLII of 1999. A proven breach will incur a fee of EUR 200, and any fine imposed on the Hotel may be recovered from the guest responsible.
  • Pets are not permitted in the building.
  • Flammable or explosive materials may not be brought into the building.
  • In the event of a fire alarm, notify reception immediately and follow the evacuation instructions.
  • Children under the age of 16 must be supervised by an adult at all times.
  • Commercial photography, filming, vlogging or live streaming on the premises requires the Hotel's prior written permission.
  • CCTV is in operation in common areas, but never in guest rooms, bathrooms or changing areas.

09Refusal or termination of a stay

The Hotel may refuse service, or terminate a stay with immediate effect, where a guest:

  • behaves in an abusive or threatening manner towards staff, other guests or third parties;
  • misuses the room or the building's facilities;
  • breaches the safety rules or house rules, or guest is intoxicated to the extent of disturbing others;
  • refuses to present identification or to complete the statutory registration; or
  • fails to agree on house rules presented at check-in; or
  • fails to make a payment by its due date.

In such cases, the accommodation fee will not be refunded.

10Inability to provide the booked room

If, for reasons attributable to the Hotel, the booked accommodation cannot be provided, the Hotel will arrange a room of the same or a higher category nearby at the confirmed rate, cover any price difference and the cost of the transfer, and provide the guest with the means to inform anyone who needs to be notified.

If the booking cannot be honored for reasons beyond the Hotel's control, the Hotel will inform the guest promptly. The guest may either accept an equivalent alternative on the same terms, or cancel the booking without penalty and receive a full refund.

11Liability

The Hotel is liable for damage caused to a guest within the building by the Hotel or its staff. The Hotel is not liable for damage arising from events beyond its control or caused by the guest.

Any damage or loss must be reported to reception immediately, together with the information needed to investigate it. Claims raised only after departure cannot be properly examined and will not be accepted.

The Hotel's liability for valuables is limited to items deposited in the in-room safe. The Hotel accepts no responsibility for belongings left in public areas.

Guests are responsible for any damage caused by themselves, their companions or anyone in their care. The Hotel may take a pre-authorisation on the guest's card as security against damage or additional consumption; the 200 EUR amount is blocked rather than charged, and is released if not used.

The restaurant, the GoActive gym and the Create 26 coworking floor in the building are operated by independent companies, each of which is a contractual partner of Secret Hotel Kft. These operators provide their services in their own name and are solely responsible for them. The Hotel will gladly arrange access, but any complaints regarding those services are handled by the operator concerned.

12Complaints

Please report any problem to reception during your stay, when it can usually be resolved on the spot. Written complaints are investigated and answered within 30 days.

13Force majeure

Neither party shall be in breach of these Terms where performance is prevented by an event beyond its control, such as war, fire, flood, severe weather, power failure, epidemic, strike or a comparable event. The affected party must notify the other party without delay and take reasonable steps to limit the consequences.

These Terms are governed by Hungarian law. Any disputes shall fall under the jurisdiction of the court competent for the place where the service is provided.
