02Key terms

Personal data — any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

Processing — any operation performed on personal data: collection, recording, storage, alteration, use, disclosure, erasure and so on.

Controller — the party that determines the purposes and means of processing. For the activities described here, that is us.

Processor — a party that processes personal data on our instructions, such as our reservation system provider.

Data subject — you: a guest, a contractual partner, someone who contacts us, or a job applicant.

03Principles

We process personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently; only for specified and legitimate purposes; only to the extent necessary; accurately and kept up to date; for no longer than needed; and with appropriate security.

04What we process, and why

4.1 Room reservation

To make and honour a booking we process your name, e-mail address, telephone number, address, billing details, arrival and departure dates, room type, number of guests, payment method, and any preference or request you tell us about (dietary needs, arrival time, accessibility requirements).

Where you book through a travel agency or an online travel platform, that party transfers the booking data to us.

Legal basis: performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b).
Retention: five years after the relationship ends, under section 6:22 of the Civil Code. Where the data forms part of accounting documentation, eight years under section 169 of Act C of 2000 on Accounting.

4.2 The reservation and booking engine

Our online booking runs on SabeeApp, operated by thePass Kft. When you open the booking engine from our site, the dates and guest count you entered may be passed to it so you do not have to type them again. The booking itself is completed in SabeeApp under its own terms.

Legal basis: performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b).

4.3 Statutory guest registration (VIZA)

Hungarian law requires every accommodation provider to record the identity document of each guest at check-in and to transmit the data to the VIZA closed guest information system. We use a document reader for this. Without a photo identity document we cannot accommodate you.

The data recorded: surname and forename, birth name, place and date of birth, sex, nationality, mother's birth name, identity or travel document data and its scanned image, visa or residence permit number for third-country nationals, date and place of entry, the address of the accommodation, and the start and end dates of the stay.

Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c), under Act CLVI of 2016.
Retention: until the last day of the first year after the data is recorded; the VIZA system retains transmitted data for up to two years.

4.4 Payment and card data

To guarantee a reservation or take payment we process the cardholder name, card number and expiry date. Card data entered in the booking engine is handled by the payment provider — we do not store full card numbers on our website.

Legal basis: performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b).
Retention: card guarantee data is deleted no later than eight calendar days after departure.

4.5 Contacting us

If you write to us through the contact form, by e-mail, by telephone or over WhatsApp, we process your name, e-mail address, telephone number where given, the subject you select and the content of your message, so that we can answer you. The form also records which language version of the site you used, so we can reply in that language.

Legal basis: your consent, Article 6(1)(a), or the steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract, Article 6(1)(b).
Retention: until you withdraw your consent, or for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry. Write to info@storiesbudapest.com to withdraw it.

4.6 Invoicing

We issue invoices under Hungarian tax law and process the data those require: name, billing address and, for business invoices, tax number.

Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c).
Retention: eight years, section 169 of the Accounting Act.

4.7 Complaints

If you complain, we record your name, contact details, the substance of the complaint and our response.

Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c), under the Consumer Protection Act.
Retention: five years.

4.8 CCTV

Cameras operate in the common areas of the building for the protection of persons and property. There are no cameras in guest rooms, bathrooms or changing areas. Signage marks the monitored areas.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f), in protecting guests, staff and property.
Retention: as permitted by law; recordings are deleted automatically when the period expires unless needed for a specific case.

4.9 Lost property

Where we find an item and can identify its owner, we process the name and contact details needed to return it.

Legal basis: legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f).

06Transfers outside the EEA

We do not transfer your personal data outside the European Economic Area for our own purposes.

Two technical exceptions are worth naming plainly. Our website is hosted by Netlify, a US company, which processes data under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, and the neighbourhood map from CARTO and unpkg; in each case your browser makes a direct request to those services, which will see your IP address. No booking, guest or contact data is sent to them.

07Cookies and the website

This site runs no analytics and no advertising. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no remarketing tag and no third-party tracking cookie anywhere on it. We do not build profiles, and no automated decision-making takes place.

The site stores exactly two values in your browser, both strictly functional:

WhatWhereWhy
storiesWelcomedlocalStorageRemembers that you have seen the opening animation, so returning visitors skip it. Holds no personal data.
ptsessionStorageCarries the page-transition state from one page to the next during a single visit. Cleared when you close the tab.

Because neither is used for tracking, advertising or profiling, they fall outside the consent requirement and no cookie banner is shown.

Your browser also contacts three external services while rendering the site: Google Fonts for typefaces, unpkg for the map library, and CARTO for map tiles on the neighbourhood page — the last two only if you scroll to the map. Those services receive your IP address as part of an ordinary web request, and their own privacy terms apply. You can block them in your browser; the site remains usable.

Opening the booking engine takes you to SabeeApp, and following a social or review link takes you to that platform. Those are separate services with their own cookies and privacy policies.

08Children

We do not knowingly process the personal data of anyone under 16 on the basis of their own consent. A person under 14 may only be represented by a parent or guardian; between 14 and 18, a legal representative's consent is required. Where a child stays with us, the data required by law is recorded on the basis of the accompanying adult's statement.

09Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk: encrypted transmission, access limited to staff who need it, and training for those who handle guest data. Our website is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we take the protection of your data seriously and review our measures.

10Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we process data about you, and give you a copy — right of access (Article 15)
  • correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete — rectification (Article 16)
  • delete your data where there is no longer a lawful reason to keep it — erasure (Article 17). This cannot override a statutory retention duty, such as accounting or VIZA records
  • limit what we do with your data while a question about it is resolved — restriction (Article 18)
  • provide your data in a portable, machine-readable form — portability (Article 20)
  • stop processing based on our legitimate interest — objection (Article 21)
  • withdraw a consent you have given, at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of what came before (Article 7)

Write to info@storiesbudapest.com or to Király utca 26, 1061 Budapest. We answer within one month; if a request is complex we may extend that by two further months and will tell you why.
