## Design Specificity Verdict

**Craft high, specificity low — and the one unforgeable asset is invisible on mobile.**

Strip the photography and swap six room names and this works unchanged for two hundred European boutique hotels. Every h2 on all seven pages is the identical `Word <em>word.</em>` construction. The interaction vocabulary is borrowed and CLAUDE.md names the sources: the chapter rail is lukebaffait.fr, the mobile selector is "alma-style", the intro fill is "tresmarescapital-style".

Two things are authored for this hotel: the coffee to Danube morph, and the seven walking destinations with times — which PRODUCT.md calls the argument. That block renders at opacity 0 on every phone.

**Deterministic scan:** 34 findings across 6 rules. layout-transition 7 (true, trivial), em-dash-overuse 5 (style heuristic, not assessable), marquee 1 (documented, intentional), dark-glow 7 (split — the map pin glow is real, the nav-cta hover lift is a legitimate elevation cue), codex-grid-background 7 (false positive — it matched the film-grain dot texture, not a blueprint grid), radial-halo 7 (false positive — it matched the emerald tint on the hidden mobile menu overlay). No framework rules misfired. Net: roughly 8 findings worth acting on out of 34.

**Browser overlays:** not available. The preview pane accepts DOM mutation but reports visibilityState hidden and innerWidth 0, so every geometric measurement from it is meaningless. All layout, viewport and animation findings were declared unreliable and dropped rather than guessed. Asset integrity was re-checked statically instead: 170 unique local references, 0 missing.

## Overall Impression

The engineering is well above the norm for a hotel site and the writing in faqs.html is the most trustworthy thing here. But the site cannot take a booking, cannot state a price, and publishes a review score the product record forbids. The biggest opportunity is not more craft — it is making the existing craft point at a working reservation.

## What's Working

**The coffee to Danube scene is a real editorial idea.** One canvas for two frame sets with fractional-index alpha blending so the scrub never steps; the breakfast-to-neighbourhood handover at p=0.62 that the chapter rail then reuses. It works because the transition *is* the argument: this house's breakfast belongs to this city.

**The FAQ copy.** It states the historic building's step-free limits honestly and refuses to bundle breakfast on principle. This is the brand voice working — concrete, first person plural, declining to oversell.

**The mobile room selector.** The display:contents dissolve that turns a desktop sidebar into a sticky pill bar keeps six choices reachable through a long photo scroll with no modal. The 86px-to-0 sync with the hiding nav is considered work.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The trust grid publishes invented proof**
The Reviews card reads "Four point eight, quietly earned / Twelve hundred guests have stayed and written it down". Also "Lowest rate, guaranteed" and "From €55 each way". Verified present.
Why: PRODUCT.md lists review scores, customer counts and rates as absent and forbids inventing them. A published aggregate rating a hotel cannot substantiate is an unfair commercial practice under EU consumer law, and faqs.html contradicts the rate guarantee on the same site.
Fix: delete the Reviews card; replace with an owned claim. Match the rate wording to the FAQ. Remove the €55 figure until a real price exists.
Command: /impeccable clarify

**[P0] The neighbourhood list and map are invisible on mobile and reduced motion**
body.bk-static restores .dn-photo, .dn-sky-video, .dn-sky, .bk-coffee-content and .dn-content — but never .dn-detail, which keeps opacity: var(--dn-detail-opacity, 0) while the controller returns early. Verified.
Why: below 860px the homepage silently deletes the content PRODUCT.md calls the argument. A phone visitor reads "Seven short steps from the door" and is shown nothing.
Fix: add .dn-detail to the bk-static block with position:relative, inset:auto, opacity:1, pointer-events:auto, and place it after .dn-content in source order.
Command: /impeccable adapt

**[P1] Both forms are dead and both promise a reply**
index reserve form and contact form are onsubmit="event.preventDefault()". Copy above each promises a same-day answer.
Why: success for this product is a completed direct reservation. A guest who fills either form believes they contacted the hotel and will not try again. This is worse than no form — no form would send them to the working footer mailto.
Fix: Netlify Forms — name, data-netlify, honeypot, success page. Add name/email and a hidden room field fed from a ?room= param the six room CTAs should append. Add min on check-in and a guard so check-out cannot precede it. Convert span.lab to label for.
Command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] faqs.html is a total keyboard lockout**
24 non-focusable controls, all on this page: 19 div.faq-q accordion triggers and 5 span.faq-nav-item category jumps. No tabindex, no role, no aria-expanded. Closed answers are display:none, so 24 of 25 answers are absent from the accessibility tree with no way to open them without a mouse.
Why: WCAG AA is a confirmed product requirement, and this is the page a screen-reader user most needs — it is the only place the building's step-free limits are stated.
Fix: button type=button with aria-expanded and aria-controls; hidden on the panel instead of display:none; real anchors for the category nav routed through Lenis. Drop the exclusive-close behaviour. Replace the hidden sidebar below 1024px with the sticky bar pattern that already works in .room-list.
Command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] The mono label system fails AA everywhere**
--text-3 #9A9A8C on --paper is 2.31:1. --text-2 #6A6A5C on paper is 4.45:1. --accent #B9904E on paper is 2.38:1. All at 10-11px, so no large-text exemption applies. This system carries the phone number, room dimensions, opening hours and every form field name.
Fix: retune two tokens rather than 40 rules — --text-3 to about #6E6E60 and --text-2 to about #5A5A4E. Never use --accent for text on paper; use --primary at 10.9:1. Lift the mono floor from 10px to 11px.
Command: /impeccable audit

**[P2] Keyboard focus lands inside invisible layers**
.bk-coffee-content and .dn-detail sit at opacity 0 but stay in the tab order; pointer-events is toggled by scroll progress, focusability is not. Tabbing out of the rooms cinema puts focus on a transparent link inside a 1200vh sticky section.
Fix: mirror the pointerEvents writes with inert in setSceneProgress, and clear inert under bk-static.
Command: /impeccable harden
